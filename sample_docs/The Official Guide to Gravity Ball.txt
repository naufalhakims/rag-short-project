The Official Guide to Gravity Ball (Season 2026)

1. Overview
Gravity Ball is a high-intensity orbital sport played in zero-gravity environments, specifically within the "Aegis Spheres" located on the Lunar Surface. The game consists of two teams of four players attempting to launch a magnetized obsidian sphere into the opposing team’s "Vortex Goal."

2. Equipment

    The Sphere: A 5kg ball made of polished obsidian with a neodymium core.

    Pulse Mitts: Specialized gloves that emit short-range kinetic blasts to repel or attract the sphere.

    Aero-Suits: Pressurized suits equipped with six micro-thrusters for navigation.

3. Scoring System

    Standard Goal: 2 points.

    The 'Long-Shot' (beyond 20 meters): 5 points.

    The 'Eclipse': If a player passes the ball through the center ring before scoring, the points are doubled.

4. Key Rules and Penalties

    Thruster Overheat: Players cannot use continuous thrust for more than 4 seconds. Doing so results in a "Dead Drift" penalty (30 seconds of sitting out).

    No Physical Contact: Pulse Mitts are for the ball only. Touching an opponent results in a turnover.

    The 'Singularity' Rule: During the final 2 minutes, the goal shrinks to half its size, but all points are tripled.

5. Famous Teams
The current reigning champions are the Neptune Knights, led by their star striker, Kaelen Voss, who holds the record for the most 'Eclipse' shots in a single season.
